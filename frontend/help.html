Quick Solutions

📦 My order hasn't arrived

Open Track Order to check the latest delivery status. If the estimated time has passed, contact our support team.

💳 Payment was deducted but order failed

Check your order history first. If the order was not created, contact support with your payment reference.

↩️ Where is my refund?

Check your refund status under My Orders. Processing time may depend on your payment provider.

🎁 My coupon is not working

Check the offer's minimum order value, expiry date, and eligibility requirements before applying it.

👤 I cannot login

Check your username and password. If the problem continues, use the account recovery option or contact our support team.

🛵 My delivery is delayed

Check the live order status first. If your order remains delayed, contact the Help Centre for assistance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order?

You can easily track your order from the Track Order section in your Thrifty Bite account. It shows the current status of your order, including confirmation, preparation, and delivery. You can also view the estimated delivery time and order details. If the order is delayed, contact our support team for further assistance.

Can I cancel my order?

You may be able to cancel your order depending on its current preparation status. Open My Orders and select the order you want to cancel. If the cancellation option is available, follow the instructions shown on the screen. Once the cancellation is confirmed, the applicable refund process will begin automatically.

What should I do if I receive the wrong item?

If you receive an incorrect food item, open your order details and select the support option. Provide the order number and explain the issue clearly to our support team. You may also be asked to provide a photo of the received item for verification. Our team will review the issue and help you with the appropriate resolution as quickly as possible.

How do Royal Rewards work?

Royal Rewards allow you to collect Royal Points through eligible activities and orders on Thrifty Bite. Your available points can be viewed from the Rewards section of your account. Depending on the available offers, points may be used for eligible benefits, discounts, or special rewards. Check the Rewards section regularly for new benefits.

How can I contact Thrifty Bite?

You can contact the Thrifty Bite Help Centre whenever you need assistance with your order or account. Call our support number provided at the bottom of this page for direct help. You can also contact us through the support email provided in the Help Centre. Keep your order number ready so our team can identify your issue quickly and provide the right solution.

What should I do if my payment was deducted?

If money has been deducted but your order was not successfully placed, first check your My Orders section. Sometimes the payment confirmation may take a short time to update. If the order does not appear, keep your payment transaction details ready and contact our Help Centre. Our team will verify the transaction and guide you through the refund or resolution process.

What can I do if my delivery is delayed?

First, open the Track Order section to check the latest delivery status. Delivery times can occasionally change because of traffic, weather, restaurant preparation, or other unexpected situations. If the order has exceeded the estimated delivery time, contact our Help Centre for assistance. Please keep your order number available when contacting the support team.

Why is my coupon or discount not working?

Check whether your coupon is still valid and whether your order meets the required minimum order value. Some offers may only be available for selected restaurants, users, or payment methods. Also make sure that the coupon code has been entered correctly without extra spaces. If the offer should apply but does not work, contact our Help Centre for further assistance.
