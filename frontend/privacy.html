THRIFTY BITE • YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

1. Information We Collect

When you use Thrifty Bite, we may collect information such as your email address, account information, order information and preferences required to provide the service.

Passwords should never be shared with other people. Passwords are securely processed by the application's backend and should not be stored in plain text.

4. Payment Information

Where third-party payment services are used, payment information may be processed by the relevant payment provider according to its own privacy and security policies.

The application may use browser storage technologies such as localStorage or sessionStorage for features such as maintaining login sessions, cart information and temporary preferences.

6. Third-Party Services

Certain features may depend on third-party services such as payment providers, maps, email services, restaurant services or other technology providers. Those services may have their own privacy policies.

7. Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

8. User Responsibility

Users should ensure that their account information remains accurate and should immediately report suspicious activity through the available support channels.

9. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, features or applicable requirements change. Updated versions will replace previous versions.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please contact Thrifty Bite through the Help Centre.
